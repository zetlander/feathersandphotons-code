**Porkbun DNS Setup**

* Log in to Porkbun and navigate to the DNS management page for your domain.
* Delete any default A, ALIAS, or CNAME records Porkbun may have automatically generated during purchase.
* Add an **A Record** with the Host set to `@` (or leave it blank) and the Answer/Value set to Netlify's load balancer IP: `75.2.60.5`.
* Add a **CNAME Record** with the Host set to `www` and the Answer/Value pointing directly to your Netlify project: `thriving-muffin-ea066f.netlify.app`.

**SSL Certificate Provisioning**

* Once the DNS records propagate across the internet (which typically takes just a few minutes), return to your Netlify tab.
* Scroll down to the **HTTPS** section to ensure Netlify has automatically provisioned a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate, ensuring visitors can securely browse your coastal wildlife and deep-sky image collections. If the certificate provisioning is pending, click **Verify DNS configuration** to force a check.
